**Sausage and Potatoes** — Any Meal

When you only have potatoes and sausages. Onions are good too, but the name would be too long.

## Steps

1. **Prep your veg.** Wash potatoes. Peel if desired.

2. **Cook the potatoes.** Throw potatoes in a pan and hit em with some water. Don't cover them. Just enough water. Let it steam. Oil and season those bastards once the water is mostly gone. Onions. Peppers.

3. **Add the sausage.** Slice sausage now that your board is cleared and toss in. Garlic — oh yeah there is garlic and peppers — whatever you have.

4. **Eat.**

## The Philosophy

This is a real meal for someone before work or lunch or even dinner. You take a starch. Cook it. Add some tough vegetables, add some cooking meat.

The order: **Optional Sear → Starch/hearty veg → hearty spices → soft veg → meat → spices (garlic) → leaves (herbs)**

## Pan Sauce (Optional)

- Remove the bulk from the pan
- If lots of fond on the pan, deglaze, mustard, seasonings, butter
- You can cook a countably infinite number of meals using this operation. Skip all you want.
