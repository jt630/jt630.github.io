The name is Josh. You already knew that.

Born and raised. Firefighter. Farmer. Cook. In that order or none at all depending on the time of year. The engine rolls when the engine rolls. The almonds don't care what month it is. The kitchen is always open because somebody's always hungry and that somebody is usually me.

---

The truck is a Type 5. Engine 4501. She's been up the 43 road and down the 40 road and she'll go wherever she's pointed. That's the deal with 4501. You point her somewhere and you hold on.

I drive barefoot when it's wet and I've been told that's a character flaw. It isn't. It's a preference. There's a difference and Tuzaga will never understand it.

---

The Yankees are going to win the World Series. I've been saying this for years and the math is going to work out eventually. That's how math works. That's also how faith works. Caminero hit a home run. It's a process.

The Mets are in trouble and Shohei knows it. Everyone knows it. You don't have to say it out loud for it to be true.

---

I drink Downeast ciders when it's warm and whiskey when it isn't. Sometimes both. The line between warm and not warm is negotiable and that's intentional. Life is better when the categories are loose.

The AC went out in April because a neighbor watched a short circuit for over an hour and didn't flip the breaker. Just watched it. An hour is a long time to watch something go wrong without doing anything about it. I think about that more than I probably should.

---

I've met a lot of people on fire rolls. Justin from Walnut, Mississippi. Fidel from Enterprise, Oregon. Tuzaga from Southern California by way of everywhere he's been stationed. Connor Rupert, who was right about the warming hut when Tuzaga wasn't.

Every fourteen days you meet someone new and that's not nothing. Most people go their whole lives without spending fourteen days sleeping next to strangers in a mountain that's actively on fire. I've done it enough times that the math starts to feel like a gift.

---

The farm is almonds. Always almonds. There's a lifecycle to it and the lifecycle doesn't negotiate. You're either on it or behind it and behind it is a bad place to be. I'm on it. Usually.

---

The kitchen is serious. If you eat at my table you eat well. That's a promise and promises mean something. I didn't learn to cook from a recipe. I learned from paying attention, which is the only way to learn anything real.

---

Lincoln said it's better to keep your mouth shut and let people presume you're ignorant than to open it and remove all doubt. My father said it before I knew it was Lincoln. Now I say it and sometimes I say where it came from and sometimes I don't.

---

This is Josh.

This is the whole deal.

Born and raised. Point the truck. The math works out. The almonds don't negotiate. The cider's cold.

Happy to be here.
