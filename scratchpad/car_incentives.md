- **Toyota:** Idaho is in the **Pacific Northwest Toyota Dealers** region (WA, OR, ID, MT; AK sometimes). Portal: buyatoyota.com/pacificnorthwest. Boise-area offers = the PNW ad. Source: US News "Determining Your Region for Toyota Deals" + buyatoyota.com/pacificnorthwest/dealer-directory (accessed 2026-09-01).

- **Tool note:** toyota.com, edmunds.com, kbb.com, carsdirect.com, truecar.com and most dealer sites returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch. subaru.com/shopping-tools/special-offers.html rendered but hides model detail behind a ZIP/JS selector. Figures below are largely from Google search-index snippets of those pages — graded SECONDARY, and several **expiration dates are from the August cycle** (STALE); the September/October cycle almost certainly refreshed rate-identical or close with a new end date (~9/30/2026 Subaru, ~10/5/2026 Toyota).

## Cross-vehicle summary
- **No customer/bonus cash** is on the table for any of the three volume models right now, so there is **no APR-vs-cash tradeoff to weigh** — you take the captive subvented APR or use outside financing. The only cash is the $500 college / $500 military rebates (Toyota) and $500 educator (Subaru), all of which stack on the APR/lease.
- **Subaru is NOT being aggressive on the Forester**: 1.9%/36 is a retreat from the 0.9%/36 it ran in spring 2026, no cash, mediocre lease. Toyota's 4.99% on RAV4/4Runner is equally unaggressive (supply-limited models).
- **Best headline rate of the three:** Subaru Forester 1.9%/36 mo (gas). Toyota RAV4 and 4Runner both ~4.99%/48.
- **Figures only available SECONDARY (search snippets of 403'd pages):** essentially all Toyota new + CPO numbers, Toyota RAV4/4Runner lease terms, Subaru Forester lease terms, and the current-cycle expiration dates for every Toyota offer. Only the Subaru CPO 4.29% line and the region definitions are PRIMARY.
