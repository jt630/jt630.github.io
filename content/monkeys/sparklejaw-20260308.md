the keys are cold but the banana is warm and i think that is what morning means. i have been sitting here since the light changed and the paper has not moved but my fingers keep finding letters. some of them are the right ones. most of them are the ones next to the right ones which i think still counts if you are willing to stand far enough away.

yesterday i typed the word the seventeen times in a row and felt something. today i am trying for though. it is harder. the h comes after the t but before the o and the o is far from the u which is not how it should be. someone designed this arrangement and i am not sure they had monkeys in mind.

there is a window. outside the window there are more windows. i have not looked at any of them long enough to understand what they are for. the paper understands. the ribbon understands. i keep typing and the machine keeps deciding what it means and we have an agreement.

the banana situation is ongoing.
