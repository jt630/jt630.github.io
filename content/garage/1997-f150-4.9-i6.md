The 4.9L (300 cu in) inline-six in these trucks basically doesn't die — but the
trucks around it are 25+ years old and it shows. Running log for this one.

## Current punch list

- [ ] **Left mirror shakes** — almost always loose mounting hardware, not the
  mirror itself. Pull the door panel/mirror trim triangle and check the three
  mounting bolts/nuts at the base — they back out from vibration over the
  years. If it's still loose with the bolts tight, the mirror bracket itself
  (or, on a power mirror, the motor's plastic gear housing) has worn out and
  needs replacing, not just tightening.
- [ ] **Belt squeaks** — check the serpentine belt for glazing/cracking first
  (shiny, hard, or cracked = replace it, don't just dress it). If the belt
  looks fine, the noise is usually the tensioner — either the tensioner
  pulley bearing is going, or spring tension is weak and it's letting the
  belt slip under load. Spin the tensioner pulley by hand with the belt off;
  any grind or roughness = replace the tensioner.
- [ ] **AC blowing/freezing solid** — classic symptom on these of the
  low-pressure cycling switch failing, or refrigerant running low so the
  low side drops below freezing and ices the evaporator instead of just
  cooling it. Check charge pressure first; if it's fine, the cycling switch
  (mounted on the accumulator) is the next suspect. Don't just keep running
  it iced up — it'll eventually starve the compressor of oil.
- [ ] **Shifter plastic broken** — this is the famous one. The shift lever
  pivots on a nylon bushing at the base that gets brittle and snaps with age,
  giving you sloppy/broken-feeling shifts. It's a ~$5 part (base O-ring/bushing
  kit) and a well-documented DIY swap — no need to replace the whole shifter
  assembly.

## Known issues on 4.9L I6 F150s (reference)

Stuff to watch for even if it's not acting up yet:

- **Exhaust manifold cracks/leaks** — the manifold bolts break off and the
  manifold itself cracks, causing a tick that gets louder with the engine
  cold. Common enough that it's basically a maintenance item, not a defect.
- **TFI ignition module heat soak** — mounted right on the distributor,
  cooks itself from engine heat and causes stalling/no-start when hot.
  Cheap insurance to relocate it with a heat sink extension if it hasn't
  failed yet.
- **Vacuum leaks** — 25-year-old vacuum lines get brittle and crack, causing
  rough idle or stalling. Worth doing a full hose inspection/replacement
  pass if idle quality is off.
- **Twin I-beam front end wear** — ball joints, idler arm, and pitman arm
  wear out and show up as wander/looseness in the steering. Check play at
  the wheel and under the truck periodically.
- **Rust** — cab corners, rear wheel arches, and frame rails are the spots
  to actually get under the truck and look at.
- **Door sag** — door hinge pins/bushings wear and the doors drop over time.
  Fixable with a hinge pin/bushing kit instead of new hinges.
- **Heater core leaks** — coolant smell/fog on the inside of the windshield
  is the tell.
