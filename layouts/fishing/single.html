Sources: USGS gauges (flow, water temp; provisional data) and the National Weather Service forecast grid plus Boise airport pressure. Both are fetched live in your browser, nothing is cached on the site. Forecast days assume today's flow holds, which is a real guess below a dam: check the 24h change before you commit to a long drive. Ratings, flow ranges and "what to throw" are starting heuristics in data/fishing.yaml, and the log below is how they get corrected.

Field log

Log a trip while you are out or right after. Saving attaches what the page predicted and the live conditions at that moment, so the rating can be checked against how the day actually fished. Entries live in this browser until you export them and paste them to Claude (/fishing-log).
