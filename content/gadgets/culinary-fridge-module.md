## The Problem

Salting and air-drying meat in the fridge for 12–48 hours gives far better steak crusts and crispier chicken skin. Current methods all fall short:

1. **Wire rack on a baking sheet** — raw meat sits exposed, so cross-contamination worry, fridge odors and drips.
2. **Airtight containers** — they trap humidity and steam the meat instead of drying it.
3. **Active dry-aging cabinets** (e.g. SteakAger PRO 15) — $300+, and built for multi-week beef aging rather than a 12–24 hour weeknight prep.

---

## The Concept

A low-profile **fridge module**, not just a lidded box. It turns an ordinary refrigerator into a dry-brining and crispy-skin prep station.

- **Height:** about 3"–3.5", so it fits a standard shelf.
- **Optional rail:** a clip-on under-shelf rail lets it hang and slide out like a deli drawer.
- **Use cases:** steaks, chicken, pork chops.

---

## V1 Build

Off-the-shelf parts plus two custom-tooled parts keep tooling cost down.

| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Pan | Commercial 1/3 or 1/2-size Gastronorm (GN), 304 stainless, dishwasher-safe, resists salt pitting |
| Rack | Lift-out wire rack that fits the GN pan |
| **Lid** (custom) | Clear Tritan, dishwasher-safe, sliding exhaust louver at one end, magnetic/snap dock on the front face |
| **Fan pod** (custom) | Detachable front-mounted module, IPX4, USB-C rechargeable, cold-rated battery for 35–40°F, quiet 40–50mm blower, snaps off in about 1 second for washing |

### Presets

| Preset | Run time |
|---|---|
| Steak | 12 h |
| Poultry / Crispy Skin | 24 h |
| Off / Vent-Only | Passive drying, or airtight marinating/storage with the louver closed |

## Open Risks

- **COGS is tight.** The pan and rack are roughly $7–12 landed, leaving about $5–8 for the lid, fan pod, battery, PCB, seals and packaging. A realistic landed cost may be $20–26, which would support a $79 MSRP.
- **Food safety.** Moving air over raw poultry in a closed lid has to be contained: lid fit, exhaust path and drip control need early testing. Avoid "kills bacteria" or "safe dry-aging" claims.
- **Cold battery and sealing.** Cold derating, the IPX4 seal and certifications (UN38.3 shipping, UL/CE/FCC) drive cost. A fixed cell or a standard replaceable cell may be cheaper than a custom pack.
