Scouting trips within a few hours of Boise. Top contenders so far:

## Seven Devils

Rugged range above Hells Canyon, west of Riggins. Big payoff views straight down into the canyon. Popular loop is the Seven Devils Loop (~19 mi) past Dry Diggins Lookout and the Devils Farm/Baldy Lakes basins. Trailhead is a longer drive from Boise (~3.5-4 hrs) but less crowded than the Sawtooths.

## Sawtooths

The classic. Alice-Toxaway Loop and the Sawtooth Lake trail are the big ones. Closer to Stanley (~2.5-3 hrs from Boise). Permits/quotas may apply for some trailheads in peak season — need to check.

Also worth noting: the west side out of Atlanta is a lot quieter than the Stanley trailheads, and permits there are free and self-issued with no quota. Full plan written up in [Queens River Loop]({{< ref "queens-river-loop" >}}).

## White Clouds

Adjacent to the Sawtooths, less trafficked. Castle Peak / Chamberlain Basin area is the highlight. Access via Stanley or the East Fork Salmon River side. Worth it if Sawtooth trailheads are too crowded/permitted out.

## McCall area

Ponderosa/Payette National Forest access, includes stuff like Loon Lake and the West Mountains. Shorter drive (~2 hrs), good backup or shorter-trip option, less alpine than the other three.

## Trip report: White Clouds Loop (modified)

Went with the White Clouds. Route:

1. **Fourth of July trailhead** — start
2. **Born Lakes** — cutthroat
3. **Four Lakes Basin** — fished Quiet Lake and Noisy Lake; best alpine berries I've ever had coming out of Noisy Lake
4. **Baker Lake** — overnight; cutthroat and possibly bull trout
5. **Chamberlain Lakes** — up and over; multiple groups of mountain goats and deer
6. Out

### Fishing notes

- All fish caught were small (Fish and Game gave the go-ahead to keep a fire and eat what we caught, for the sizes/growth we were seeing).
- Baker Lake had a lot of deadfall in the water — excellent trout fishing, also excellent snags.
- Best flies: elk hair caddis and an Adams, both small — size 18 max.

### Other notes

- No moon out, so the stars were incredible.

## Banger ideas to try

Untested, high-upside stuff — inspired by the jello setting perfectly in a cold river.

**Cold-river/snowmelt chilled**

- Instant pudding — same trick as the jello
- Cold brew concentrate — sink the bottle, pour over ice-cold "ice" from the stream
- Canned cocktails/hard seltzers, chilled the same way

**Meal upgrades that punch way above their weight**

- Mini bottle of good olive oil for finishing dehydrated meals
- Packet of parmesan or nutritional yeast
- Mini hot sauce bottle
- Fresh herbs in a ziplock (basil/cilantro) — lasts a couple days, huge flavor lift
- Freeze-dried cheesecake or cobbler kit for a dessert night

**Camp comfort**

- Small inflatable solar lantern (Luci-style) — way better ambient light than a headlamp beam
- Foam sit pad for lounging on rocks/logs
- Cribbage board or deck of cards for camp downtime

**Field tested, still not fully believed until it works:** jello set in a river = banger. See what else sets/chills the same way.
